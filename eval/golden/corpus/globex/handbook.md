# Globex Employee Handbook

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours, staff
schedule their own working time.

## Equipment
Each employee receives a laptop and one external monitor. Replacement
cycle is four years.

## Parental leave
Globex provides sixteen weeks of paid parental leave, available to all
parents regardless of gender, in the twelve months following birth or
adoption.
