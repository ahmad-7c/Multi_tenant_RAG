# Globex Security Standards

## Contractor accounts
Contractor accounts expire automatically ninety days after creation. An
extension requires the sponsoring manager to re-confirm that the
contractor is still engaged.

## Device encryption
All laptops ship with full-disk encryption enabled. A lost or stolen
device must be reported to the service desk within twenty-four hours.

## Access reviews
Every system owner reviews their access list once per quarter and
removes accounts that are no longer needed.

## Phishing
Forward suspected phishing to it-help@globex.example. Do not click
links in a message you have already reported.
