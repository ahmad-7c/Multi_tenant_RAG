# Acme Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expense reports
Expense reports must be submitted within thirty days of the end of
travel. Reports submitted after this window require written approval
from a department director before they can be reimbursed.

## Receipts
Itemised receipts are required for any single expense over 25 USD.
Meals claimed under the per-diem rate do not require receipts.

## Mileage
Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at the published federal rate.
The rate is reviewed every January and announced by the finance team.
Mileage for commuting between home and the primary office is never
reimbursable.

## Per-diem rates
Per-diem depends on the destination band:

| Destination band | Daily meals | Daily incidentals |
|------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Domestic standard | 64 USD     | 12 USD            |
| Domestic high-cost | 79 USD    | 12 USD            |
| International     | 95 USD     | 20 USD            |

## Approval thresholds
A direct manager approves expense reports up to 5,000 USD. Reports above
5,000 USD additionally require finance review. Reports above 25,000 USD
require the CFO.

## Air travel
Economy class is the default for all flights. Premium economy is
permitted for flights with a scheduled duration over eight hours.
Business class requires CFO approval regardless of duration.
